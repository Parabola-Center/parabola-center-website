Parabola Center for Law and Policy

Emerging industries shouldn’t mean emerging monopolies.

Parabola Center is a nonprofit of legal, public health, and policy professionals coming together to put people over profits. Through research and education, we provide everyone with the tools and expertise to shape emerging fields, from marijuana to artificial intelligence.

What we do

Keeping the power with the people

Research

Market studies, consumer research, and policy analysis that show who really controls emerging industries.

Policy

New Report

The Illusion of Competition

By Damian Fagon and Hedy Yang

Our new report on the Illinois market maps every active cannabis brand in Illinois to the parent company that controls it, and finds market concentration rising again. That outcome was produced by policy choices: a licensing framework that gave incumbents a multiyear head start, regulators too fragmented to intervene, and a tax structure that hits small producers hardest.
